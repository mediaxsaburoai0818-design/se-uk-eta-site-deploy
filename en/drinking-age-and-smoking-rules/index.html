Arriving and staying in the UK

Drinking age in England – smoking and other rules

In England you must be 18 to buy alcohol or to drink alcohol in a pub or restaurant. If you are 16 or 17 and with an adult, you can drink (but not buy) beer, wine or cider with a meal (GOV.UK). In the UK the minimum age for buying alcohol and tobacco is 18, and smoking is banned indoors in public places.

What is the drinking age in England? 18 for alcohol and tobacco

In the UK you must be at least 18 to buy alcohol and tobacco. The age limit applies in shops, bars, restaurants and other places that sell them.

Shops, pubs and restaurants may ask for ID proving your age, even if you look older. So it is a good idea to have valid ID to hand if you plan to buy alcohol or tobacco.

Smoking ban indoors in public places

Since the smoke-free law was introduced in 2007, smoking has been banned indoors in all enclosed public places, workplaces and on public transport.

This means pubs, restaurants and stations are smoke-free indoors. You may only smoke in designated outdoor areas.

  • Indoors: smoking banned in enclosed public places and workplaces.
  • Public transport: smoke-free.
  • Allowed: only in designated outdoor areas.

Vaping and e-cigarettes

The rules for vaping and e-cigarettes vary from place to place. In many places it is not allowed indoors, but this can differ between venues and businesses. Always follow the signs and staff instructions where you are.

Drink-driving and drinking in public

Drink-driving is strictly enforced in the UK, with set legal limits and penalties. If you plan to drive, you should know the rules — these are covered in our guide to driving in the UK.

Some local councils have also introduced zones with restrictions on drinking alcohol in public places. Look out for local signs showing such restrictions.

Bringing alcohol and tobacco into the UK

Separate customs allowances (duty-free) apply to how much alcohol and tobacco you can bring into the country. These are described in our guide to customs rules and allowances.

Frequently asked questions

What is the drinking age in England?
In England you must be 18 to buy alcohol or to drink alcohol in a pub or restaurant. If you are 16 or 17 and with an adult, you can drink (but not buy) beer, wine or cider with a meal (GOV.UK).
What is the minimum age for buying alcohol and tobacco in the UK?
You must be at least 18 to buy alcohol and tobacco in the UK. Shops and bars may ask for ID proving your age.
Can I smoke in pubs and shops?
No. Smoking is banned indoors in all enclosed public places, workplaces and on public transport. Pubs, restaurants and stations are smoke-free indoors — you may only smoke in designated outdoor areas.
Can I use an e-cigarette (vape)?
The rules for e-cigarettes and vaping vary from place to place. Often it is not allowed indoors. Always follow the signs and staff instructions where you are.
