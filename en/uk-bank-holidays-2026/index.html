Arriving and staying in the UK

UK bank holidays 2026

On a bank holiday, banks are closed, many shops have shorter opening hours and public transport may run less often. Here are the official bank holidays in England and Wales in 2026 according to GOV.UK — and what they mean for your trip.

Bank holidays in England and Wales 2026

According to GOV.UK, the following official bank holidays apply in England and Wales in 2026. Scotland and Northern Ireland partly have different dates.

DateBank holiday
1 JanuaryNew Year's Day
3 AprilGood Friday
6 AprilEaster Monday
4 MayEarly May bank holiday
25 MaySpring bank holiday
31 AugustSummer bank holiday
25 DecemberChristmas Day
28 DecemberBoxing Day (substitute day)

What is open — and what is affected?

On a bank holiday, banks are closed. Many shops and attractions are open, but often with shorter opening hours. Public transport may run a reduced service, for example a Sunday timetable.

Check opening hours and timetables in advance for the specific day you are planning, as they vary between places and businesses.

Christmas Day: almost everything is closed

Christmas Day (25 December) is special: almost everything is closed and most public transport does not run. If you are travelling over Christmas, plan your days with some margin and do not count on a normal service.

Substitute days when a bank holiday falls at the weekend

If a bank holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the day off normally moves to the next weekday (a substitute day). That is why some dates in the list above fall on a weekday after a weekend.

Before you travel to the UK

To enter the country you need a valid UK ETA, and you can follow your application with the ETA status check. On arrival you go through passport control and the eGates. If you plan to drive during your trip, read about driving in the UK.

Frequently asked questions

When are the UK bank holidays in 2026?
In England and Wales, the official bank holidays in 2026 according to GOV.UK are: 1 January (New Year's Day), 3 April (Good Friday), 6 April (Easter Monday), 4 May (Early May), 25 May (Spring), 31 August (Summer), 25 December (Christmas Day) and 28 December (substitute day for Boxing Day). Scotland and Northern Ireland partly have different dates.
Are shops open on bank holidays?
Banks are closed. Many shops and attractions are open, but often with shorter opening hours. On Christmas Day (25 December) almost everything is closed.
How is public transport affected on bank holidays?
Public transport may run a reduced service, for example a Sunday timetable. On Christmas Day most services do not run, so plan your travel days with some margin.
