UK ETA Guide

What does it cost? £20

The official price on GOV.UK is £20, paid once for 2 years.

How long is it valid? 2 years

Or until your passport expires, if that comes first. It covers multiple trips.

How long does it take? Usually 1 day

Allow up to 3 working days and do not leave it to the last minute.

What if it is refused?

There is no appeal. You would then need to apply for a visa that matches the purpose of your trip.

Do children need one?

Yes. Everyone, including infants and children, needs their own authorisation.
