ETA, visas and applying

The official GOV.UK site for applying for a UK ETA

You can apply for a UK ETA on the UK government's official website or in the official mobile app.

  1. UK ETA Guide
  2. /The official GOV.UK site for applying for a UK ETA

Official web address

Official UK ETA page: www.gov.uk/eta. The .gov.uk domain means it is an official UK government site.

How to recognise the official site

  • The web address contains gov.uk (the UK government domain)
  • The site is run by the Home Office
  • The fee is exactly £20
  • The site has the distinctive black-and-white GOV.UK design
  • It does not charge a fee for "faster" processing

How to spot a fake site

Warning signs:
  • The web address does not contain gov.uk
  • A fee significantly higher than £20
  • A promise of "guaranteed approval"
  • An offer of "faster processing"
  • Aggressive advertising or intrusive pop-up windows
  • No GOV.UK logo

Official mobile app

You can also apply in the official mobile app, "UK ETA":

  • iOS: search for "UK ETA" in the App Store (publisher: Home Office)
  • Android: search for "UK ETA" on Google Play (publisher: Home Office)

How to use it: see UK ETA App.

Other useful government pages

  • gov.uk/check-uk-visa — check whether you need a visa for the UK
  • gov.uk/standard-visitor — information on the visitor visa
