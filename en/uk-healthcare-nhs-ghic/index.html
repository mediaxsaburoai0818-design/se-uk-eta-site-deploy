Arriving and staying in the UK

Healthcare, the NHS and GHIC for visitors to the UK

What is free on the NHS, how EHIC/GHIC works for visitors from the EU, EFTA and Switzerland, why travel insurance is strongly recommended and which emergency numbers to use.

  1. UK ETA Guide
  2. /Entering the UK: entry requirements and visas
  3. /Healthcare, the NHS and GHIC for visitors to the UK

A&E and GP visits are free

On the NHS, accident and emergency (A&E) and visits to a general practitioner (GP) are free for everyone — regardless of immigration status or whether you have documents with you. This applies to initial emergency care.

Visitors from the EU, EFTA and Switzerland: EHIC/GHIC

With a valid EHIC/GHIC issued in their home country, visitors from the EU, EFTA and Switzerland can get medically necessary care during a temporary stay. Note that EHIC/GHIC does not cover all costs and does not replace travel insurance.

Visitors without EHIC/GHIC

Visitors without EHIC/GHIC may be charged for hospital treatment beyond emergency care (reportedly 150% of the standard NHS charge). Urgent care that cannot wait is always given, however, and payment is dealt with afterwards.

Travel insurance is strongly recommended

Travel insurance is strongly recommended. EHIC/GHIC does not cover everything — for example, transport home (repatriation) is not normally included. Insurance is therefore an important addition and cannot be replaced by a GHIC.

Emergency numbers and health advice

  • 999 (or 112) — the emergency number for urgent situations.
  • NHS 111 — for non-urgent medical advice.
  • Prescription medicines may involve a prescription charge (in England).

Good to know before you arrive

To enter the UK you need a valid UK ETA — you can see the status of your application with the ETA status check. If you plan to hire a car, read about driving in the UK. The general entry rules, customs allowances and transit are covered in our other guides.

Frequently asked questions

Is emergency treatment free in the UK?
A&E and GP visits are free on the NHS for everyone, regardless of immigration status or documents. Hospital treatment beyond emergency care may, however, be charged to visitors without EHIC/GHIC.
Can I use an EHIC or GHIC in the UK?
Visitors from the EU, EFTA and Switzerland with a valid EHIC/GHIC issued in their home country can get medically necessary care during a temporary stay. However, EHIC/GHIC does not cover all costs and does not replace travel insurance.
Do I need travel insurance for the UK?
Travel insurance is strongly recommended. EHIC/GHIC does not cover everything — for example, transport home (repatriation) is not normally included. Travel insurance is therefore an important addition and cannot be swapped for a GHIC.
